O2 Thorn Rain (v0.3.0 - The Boss & Storm Update) introduces perilous environmental hazards and an epic celestial boss encounter!
During storms, dangerous thorns rain from the skies, challenging surface survival. Protect yourself with the vanilla Umbrella or Ironskin Potion.
Venture deep into the tempest or craft the Storm Lightning Rod to awaken "The Storm of Four" event: defeat four ancestral tornadoes across the biomes to challenge the Eye of the Thorn Storm!
Defeat the boss to claim the Spike Dragon mount, Wall Trophy, and Treasure Bag.
