[h1]O2 Thorn Rain[/h1]

[b]O2 Thorn Rain[/b] turns ordinary rain into an adrenaline-fueled survival challenge! When rainfall starts in Terraria, deadly spikes rain down relentlessly from the sky upon the surface world.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Atmospheric Hazard:[/b] Raining thorns fall continuously from the sky during storms.
[*] [b]Dynamic Trajectory:[/b] Spike trajectories drift realistically based on the world's current wind speed.
[*] [b]Progressive Hazard Damage:[/b] Spike damage scales dynamically across world progression (Pre-Hardmode, Hardmode, Post-Plantera, Post-Golem, and Post-Moon Lord) across Normal, Expert, and Master modes.
[*] [b]Surface Protection Check:[/b] Spikes only drop on surface and sky layers, sparing underground explorers and caverns.
[*] [b]Engine Optimization:[/b] Built-in spawn pacing and global/per-player active projectile caps keep your frame rate smooth and lag-free.
[*] [b]Multiplayer Friendly:[/b] Server-authoritative logic prevents desyncs and item duplication on dedicated and host & play servers.
[/list]

[h2]Protection & Survival Mechanics[/h2]
Surviving the Thorn Rain requires preparation! Utilize vanilla tools and potions to brave the storm:

[list]
[*] [b]Vanilla Umbrella Protection:[/b]
Holding an open vanilla Umbrella ([i]ItemID.Umbrella[/i]) shields you completely from falling thorn damage!
[*] [b]Umbrella Durability System:[/b]
The Umbrella now features an exposure-based durability meter (100% to 0%). It gradually wears down while exposed to the Thorn Rain with active spikes nearby, losing 1 point every 0.5 seconds of exposure.
[*] [b]Visual Durability Bar & Tooltips:[/b]
Inspect your Umbrella to view its status: New (100%-50%), Damaged (49%-15%), Almost Broken (14%-1%), and Broken (0%). A visual durability bar is rendered directly in your inventory slot!
[*] [b]Umbrella Slime Drop:[/b]
Need an Umbrella? Vanilla Umbrella Slimes now have a 5% chance to drop the vanilla Umbrella upon defeat!
[*] [b]Ironskin Potion Immunity:[/b]
Drinking an Ironskin Potion grants absolute immunity to Thorn Rain damage while the buff is active (and spares your Umbrella from losing durability)!
[/list]
