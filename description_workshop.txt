[h1]O2 Thorn Rain[/h1]

[b]O2 Thorn Rain[/b] turns ordinary rain into an adrenaline-fueled survival challenge! When rainfall starts in Terraria, deadly spikes rain down relentlessly from the sky upon the surface world.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Atmospheric Hazard:[/b] Raining thorns fall from above during storm events.
[*] [b]Dynamic Trajectory:[/b] Spike trajectories drift realistically based on the world's current wind speed.
[*] [b]Surface Protection Check:[/b] Spikes only drop on surface and sky layers, sparing underground explorers and caves.
[*] [b]Engine Optimization:[/b] Built-in spawn pacing and global active projectile caps keep your frame rate smooth and lag-free.
[*] [b]Multiplayer Friendly:[/b] Server-authoritative logic avoids desyncs on dedicated and host & play servers.
[/list]
